Before We Arrive in Edison

Shut off the water

Turn the main valve clockwise. In many Edison homes it is near the meter in the basement or utility area.

Keep clear of power

If water is near outlets, the panel or appliances, stay away and tell the dispatcher.

Stop using drains

If a drain is backing up, no laundry, dishwasher, showers or flushing until a plumber arrives.

Move valuables up

Lift rugs, boxes and electronics off a wet floor, and photograph the damage for your insurer.
