When a water heater cannot wait for a replacement

A heater that is leaking from the tank shell, has no hot water and is more than ten years old, or is dripping from several places is ready to be replaced. Turn off the cold supply to the tank, shut off the power or gas, and call the 24/7 line. The longer a failing tank sits full of water, the more water can reach the floor.

After hours, the dispatcher will tell you what to shut off and book the first available plumber. Timing depends on the schedule and stock, so ask when you call.

Choosing the replacement: tank, tankless or hybrid

For most homes the cheapest like-for-like swap is another tank of the same fuel and a similar size. Household size and shower habits matter more than square footage, and a plumber will ask how many people are in the house and how many bathrooms run at once. A larger family sometimes does better with a tankless unit, which heats on demand and takes less floor space.

Hybrid heat pump water heaters use electricity far more efficiently than a standard electric tank but need space, air flow and a drain for condensate. They suit basements with room to spare. Our tankless service page and water heater installation page cover those routes in detail.

Hard water and the replacement decision

Middlesex Water, which serves portions of South Plainfield, lists average hardness of 157 ppm with a range of 90 to 308 ppm in its 2025 report. That works out to roughly 9 grains per gallon on average, which is hard enough to coat elements and tank floors over a decade. Heaters in hard water commonly last less long than the same model in soft water.

If you are not sure which utility serves your address, check your bill. A water softener is worth asking about when a new heater goes in, since softened water protects the new unit. The utility's annual water quality report is the place to confirm the figure for your own address.

Code and standards note

New Jersey's plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, published by the International Association of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials. The code requires a relief valve with a proper discharge, and the plumber should test it. Permit rules vary, so confirm with the South Plainfield construction office.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Turn off the cold water supply valve at the top of the tank.
  • Switch off the power at the breaker, or turn the gas control to off.
  • Do not touch hot water from a leaking tank, which can scald.
  • Move items away from the floor around the tank.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

What South Plainfield homes bring to the job

Basements are where most South Plainfield water heaters live, and basements are where heavy rain collects. After Hurricane Ida flooded homes in the borough in September 2021, a lot of heaters sitting on low floors had to be checked or replaced. A new unit can be raised on a stand to keep the burner and controls above minor water, which is worth asking for.

A plumber will also look at the water meter area and the main shutoff while the system is drained, because an aging shutoff that will not close fully is a problem you want to find on a calm afternoon. If you are replacing a heater in a rental, plumbing installation work like a new shutoff can be done in the same visit.
