What we install

The most common jobs are replacement fixtures such as sinks, toilets, tubs and faucets, new shutoff valves, pressure reducing valves, hose bibs and washing machine boxes. We also run supply and drain lines for a remodeled bathroom, a basement bath or a kitchen island.

A plumber checks that the existing pipe can serve the new fixture, that the drain slopes properly and that venting meets code before any wall is closed.

Shutoff valves and pressure regulators

Old shutoff valves that stick or weep are a common reason to call, because a valve that will not close is a risk during every repair. Replacing them is quick and gives you control in an emergency. A quarter-turn ball valve is more reliable than an old multi-turn one.

A pressure reducing valve protects pipes and appliances from high street pressure. Fixtures that bang, a heater relief valve that drips and repeated leaks across the house can all point to pressure that is too high.

Backflow protection

Backflow devices stop contaminated water from flowing backward into the drinking supply. They are common on irrigation systems, boilers and some commercial buildings. Rules and testing requirements are set by the state and the local water authority, so ask the South Plainfield construction office and your water utility what applies to you.

A licensed plumber can install the device and arrange required testing. Our commercial plumbing page covers business needs.

Code and standards note

New Jersey's plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, with state amendments. One amendment requires newly installed automatic lawn sprinklers to have a rain sensor that overrides the cycle after more than one half inch of rain.

Installs are booked from Scotch Plains too, and two common jobs have their own pages: water heater installation and toilet repair and replacement.

Planning an installation visit

Bring or send photos of the space before the visit, and have a rough idea of the fixtures you want. The plumber checks where the existing supply and drain lines are, whether the vent is adequate and whether the floor or wall needs reinforcing for a heavy fixture such as a cast iron tub.

Choosing fixtures before work starts avoids delays. A fixture that is out of stock or has an unusual rough-in size can stall a job for weeks, and a plumber can advise on models that fit the existing openings.

Remodels in older South Plainfield houses

Many South Plainfield houses were built across several decades and have been added to over time. A remodel often reveals a mix of galvanized, copper and plastic pipe, plus old shutoffs that no longer close. A plumber can modernize the connections while the walls are open, which is cheaper than doing it later.

If you find several failing lines while opening a wall, our repiping page explains when a larger replacement is the better choice. Ask the South Plainfield construction office which permits and inspections apply before the work starts.
