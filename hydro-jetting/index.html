Emergency Hydro Jetting in South Plainfield, NJ

Hydro jetting uses high-pressure water to scour the inside of a drain line, cutting grease, scale and light roots instead of just poking a hole through them. It is the fix for a main line that keeps clogging after a cable has been through.

When a backed-up main line needs jetting right away

A main line that backs up into a tub or floor drain is an emergency because wastewater is entering the house. Stop using water, keep the washing machine off and call the 24/7 line. A plumber will tell you whether a cable can clear it for now or whether a jetting visit is the better move.

Jetting is not always the first tool. If a cable cannot get through, or a camera shows a collapsed section, jetting will not fix a broken pipe and a repair is needed. Ask about after-hours timing when you call.

How hydro jetting works

A hose with a special nozzle is fed into the line through a clean-out. Water at high pressure shoots out of the nozzle in several directions and pushes the nozzle forward as the stream scours the pipe wall. The debris flushes down to the municipal sewer rather than being pushed ahead of the blockage.

Because it cleans the whole circumference of the pipe, jetting removes the grease film and scale that a cable leaves behind. That is why a jetted line often stays clear for much longer than one that was just snaked.

Jetting cast iron and older pipe

Cast iron develops scale and tubercles on the inside over decades, and jetting is a standard way to clean it. The plumber runs a camera first so that a cracked or collapsed section is not blasted open. If the pipe wall is thin, a gentler approach or a repair comes first.

Our sewer inspection page explains the camera work, and sewer line repair covers what happens if the pipe itself is the problem.

Code and standards note

Plumbing code standards treat cleanout access as a basic part of drain design, which is what lets a jetting hose reach the line. A house with no accessible cleanout may need one added before jetting.

Jetting is available in Plainfield and the other towns on our route, and a line that fails the camera check moves on to sewer line repair instead.
