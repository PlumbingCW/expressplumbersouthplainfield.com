If you cannot find or turn the main valve, say so when you call. A stuck valve is a common reason for a long flood, and the plumber can shut it off at the street or replace the valve once the water is under control.

Why pipes burst in New Jersey winters

Water expands as it freezes, and the pressure builds between the ice and a closed faucet. The pipe usually splits where the ice formed, but the leak only starts when the ice melts. That is why a burst often shows up in the afternoon after a freezing night.

Pipes in unheated basements, crawlspaces, garages and exterior walls are the most exposed. When a cold snap is forecast, open cabinet doors under kitchen and bath sinks, let a faucet drip and keep the house above about 55 degrees.

What a plumber does at the scene

The plumber confirms the water is off, finds the split, cuts out the damaged section and replaces it with new pipe and fittings. If the pipe is frozen but not yet burst, careful thawing can prevent a flood. Heat is applied slowly, because a torch can start a fire inside a wall.

Afterward, the plumber will check the rest of the run for weak spots, since a pipe that split once often has more than one thin place. For widespread failures in old lines, repiping may be the better long-term choice.

After the water stops

Move valuables, lift wet rugs and run fans. Photograph the damage before you clean up, because your insurer will want to see it. Wet drywall and insulation that stay damp longer than a day or two can grow mold, so call a drying contractor if the area is large.

Burst-pipe water can reach the basement quickly in a South Plainfield house with a finished lower level. If the water reached the basement, leak detection can confirm there is no second source.

Code and standards note

New Jersey's plumbing subcode, N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code, 2021 edition, published by the International Association of Plumbing and Mechanical Officials. Repair and replacement of supply piping falls under it, and permit rules vary, so ask the South Plainfield construction office when a repair is more than a small section.
