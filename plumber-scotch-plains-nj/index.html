Winter and spring in Union County

Freezing weather splits exposed pipes in garages, crawlspaces and outside walls, and spring thaws reveal the damage. Insulate exposed lines, open cabinet doors on cold nights and know where the main valve is. If a pipe does split, burst pipe repair explains the first five minutes.
