When a clogged drain turns into an emergency

A single slow sink can wait for a daytime visit. A clog that sends water over the rim of a tub, or one that backs up several fixtures at once, cannot. When more than one drain is acting up, the blockage is usually in the main line, and every flush or load of laundry adds water that has nowhere to go.

Call the 24/7 line at (732) 466-1098 and stop running water in the house. Pull the plug from the tub if it is filling, and keep the washing machine off. Ask about after-hours timing when you call, and the dispatcher will tell you honestly how soon a plumber can arrive.

Several fixtures back up together

If the shower gurgles when the toilet flushes, or the basement floor drain bubbles after laundry, the clog is deep in the main line.

The same sink clogs again within days

A fast return usually means grease, soap or hair has built up farther down the pipe than a plunger reaches.

One drain is slow and the rest are fine

That points to a local clog or a partial blockage in a single branch, which a cable usually clears.

Water appears in the lowest drain

A floor drain or a tub on the lowest level is where a main-line backup shows up first.

How a clog is found and cleared

The plumber starts by asking what has been going down the drain and which fixtures are affected, because the pattern narrows the location before anything is opened. For a single fixture, a hand auger or a drum machine with a cable is usually enough. The cable breaks up the blockage or hooks it so it can be pulled back out.

For main lines the plumber uses a longer cable, and when the clog returns or the cable stalls, a small camera on the end of a flexible line shows what is really inside the pipe. That is how a collapsed section, a belly or a root mass gets identified instead of guessed at. If the line is coated with grease or scale, hydro jetting cleans the pipe wall rather than just punching a hole through the blockage.

Kitchen sink clogs and what causes them

Kitchen drains clog more often than bathroom drains, and the cause is almost always cooking grease that cooled and hardened inside the trap and the horizontal run behind it. A food disposal that is overloaded or jammed can add to the problem, so a clog under a double sink sometimes needs the disposal pulled before the line can be cleared. Our garbage disposal service covers that side of it.

Chemical drain openers are tempting but they rarely clear a grease plug, and they leave the water in the pipe caustic for whoever opens the trap next. A cable or a trap cleaning is safer for the pipe and for the person doing the work.

Code and standards note

Plumbing code standards require fixtures to be trapped and vented. That is why a drain that gurgles when another fixture runs can point to a vent problem, not just a clog, and why a plumber looks at both.

The same drain cleaning visit is available in Piscataway and the other towns we serve, and a main line that keeps clogging may need a camera look before anything else.

What South Plainfield drains tell us

South Plainfield became a borough in 1926 and its housing ranges from older houses to later builds, so one street can have cast iron, clay and plastic drains. Cast iron develops rough scale on the inside over decades, and that rough surface catches grease and paper. If the home is on a tree-lined street near Cedar Brook Park, roots can also find the joints in an older sewer lateral and slow the main drain over a season or two.

A leak in a drain line, as opposed to a clog, shows up as damp drywall or a stain below a bathroom rather than slow water. If a slow drain comes with a damp ceiling or a musty smell under a tub, say so when you call and the plumber will bring the right tools. For a leak the answer is leak detection, not more cabling.

Heavy rain adds a second pattern. After a storm, a basement floor drain that backs up can mean the municipal line is surcharged, not that your pipe is blocked, and a camera look will tell the difference. The same visit can include a quick look at the sump pump if the basement gets wet.
