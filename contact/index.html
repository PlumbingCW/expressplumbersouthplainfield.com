What to have ready

Your address and cross streets, what is happening, and whether the water is off. A photo helps if you can send one by email.

The fastest way to reach us is by phone. We do not use web forms, so there is nothing to fill out and no waiting for a reply. A dispatcher answers, asks what is happening and books the earliest appointment available.

When you call, say whether this is an emergency. Burst pipes, sewage backups, flooding and no-heat calls move to the front of the line. For planned work such as a faucet repair or a water heater install, the dispatcher will offer a window that suits you.

Email works for documents, photos and non-urgent questions. For anything where water is moving, call instead. Our services page lists the work we handle, and the service area page lists the towns we reach.
